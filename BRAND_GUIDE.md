# ForgeBuild Brand Guide v1.0

## Brand position

ForgeBuild is a professional parametric commercial-building modeling and delivery platform for
schools, healthcare, government, institutional, retail, warehouse, and light-industrial work.
It belongs to the same Forge product family as ForgeCase while retaining its own product identity.

## Core identity

- Product name: **ForgeBuild**
- Descriptor: **Commercial Building Solutions**
- Tagline: **Built for What’s Next**
- Personality: industrial, modern, precise, dependable, engineered, and professional

## Colors

- Forge Black: `#2D3036` — wordmark, text, geometry, and primary structure
- Forge Bronze: `#A77747` — restrained accents, highlights, buttons, and headings
- White: `#FFFFFF` — clean architectural background
- Light Gray: `#E9ECEF` — panels, tables, controls, and secondary surfaces

## Typography and interface

- Headings and wordmarks: Montserrat or Arial Black fallback
- Interface text: Inter, Source Sans Pro, or Arial fallback
- Use square two-pixel controls and panels, fine gray rules, bronze focus states, and minimal shadows.
- Use the angular ForgeBuild mark with the full wordmark in primary headers; never substitute the
  ForgeCase product name or cabinet-specific descriptor.
